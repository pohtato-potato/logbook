Logbook · look and feel · round 6

Logbook Pinboard 6

Round 5 got an 8.5. This round goes back to the full feature list and gives every item a place, with photos on Today first. It also redoes the day page five ways.

  • Photos on Today, from your own phone, plus grateful for, the song of the week, today’s stamps and the voices.
  • Feelings inside your line: type : and a word.
  • The + button now holds every kind of entry: films and books on the 1 to 7 scale, quotes, places, people, keepsakes, voice notes, spans and big moments from before.
  • A much bigger dictionary (mid, pooped and agog all work now), and your own words.
  • Tap a day on the year. Also new: shelves filled in, Almanac headlines and Wrapped, a life timeline, and settings.
  • The day page, five ways, with colours blended so they no longer end abruptly.
  • Proud, Curious, Calm and Warm redone in both form sets.
  • New screens: sharing in from other apps, the first run, and the laptop layout. At the end, a map of every feature and where it lives.
  • You loved both Atkinson and Archivo for numbers, so big figures now use Archivo and everything else stays Atkinson.
