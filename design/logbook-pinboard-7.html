Logbook Pinboard 7

Round 7 of the look and feel. It does everything from the design critique at once, together with your notes on round 6.

  • Readable first. Nothing in the phone is smaller than 14 pixels, main text is 17, everything you tap is at least 44 pixels, and all of it grows with your phone’s text size. Try Text size and Greyscale in the bar below.
  • Keeping is safer. The Keep button stays in reach and says what it will keep. Right now and The whole day both work. Every Keep can be undone, and any entry can be changed or removed.
  • Feelings in your line open a card with the word’s meaning, and can be removed.
  • Colour never works alone. The calendar shows forms, and the text on every colour is checked. Tags and marks keep the colours you loved; people and ratings step back.
  • Looking back describes and never judges. The forecast is gone, except as one joke in the Conspiracy theorist’s voice.
  • From your notes: Warm and Tense swapped, Fluid and Horizon gone, Bloom smoothed (and a version with an outline), Health’s postcard, and the laptop reworked as a reading room.
